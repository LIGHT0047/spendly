Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Spendly collects, uses, and protects information when you use our personal expense tracking application. By using Spendly, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here.

1. Data We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account information — your name and email address, provided when you register.
  • Expense records — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you enter into the app.
  • Usage information — basic information about how you interact with the app, such as the pages you visit.

Spendly does not collect bank credentials, card numbers, or access to your bank accounts. Expense data is entered manually by you.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the Spendly service.
  • Display your expenses, summaries, and category breakdowns back to you.
  • Personalize your experience and improve the app's features.
  • Send service-related notices, such as account or security updates.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use your expense records for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored on secure servers and is accessible only to you through your account. You are responsible for keeping your login credentials confidential.

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service. You may delete your expense records or account at any time, after which the associated data is removed from our active systems. We may keep limited backups for a short period for recovery purposes.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly may rely on third-party service providers for hosting, font delivery, and analytics. These providers process data on our behalf only to the extent necessary to provide their services.

These third parties have their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to review them. Spendly does not share your expense records with third parties except where required by law or as described in this policy.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal data, please contact us at support@spendly.example.
